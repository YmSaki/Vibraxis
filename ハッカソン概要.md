#### OpenAI Build Week Challenge

GPT-5.6 is here, and Codex—the same powerful coding agent—is now available in ChatGPT.OpenAI Build Week is a chance to explore what's possible with GPT-5.6 and Codex. Experiment with new ideas, connect with other builders, and submit a project for a chance to win prizes and recognition.

Submissions are due **Tuesday, July 21 at 5:00 PM PT**.

#### How to participate

1.   **Read the [Official Rules](https://openai.devpost.com/rules)**. Review the challenge requirements, eligibility, and submission guidelines.

2.   **Install the Devpost Hackathons Plugin.**Access the challenge details, rules, and submission flow directly from the ChatGPT desktop or mobile app.

3.   **Get set up with OpenAI.**[Sign up for an OpenAI account](https://auth.openai.com/create-account) then request your free Codex credits on the Resources tab by Friday, July 17th at 12:00 PM PT.

4.   **Explore the docs.**Learn about [GPT-5.6](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model?model=gpt-5.6) and Codex with the available [quickstart](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/quickstart) before you begin.

5.   **Pick your track and build.**Find the track that best fits your idea, then submit your project by**Tuesday**, **July 21 at 5:00 PM PT**.

#### **Meet the Devpost plugin**

The Devpost Hackathons plugin runs inside ChatGPT and includes everything you need to participate—from challenge requirements and rules to the submission flow. It can help you brainstorm ideas, plan your project, and prepare your submission without leaving Codex.

[![Image 1](https://devpost-public.s3.amazonaws.com/PM%20Toolkit/get-plugin-large.png)](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6a330a7730c081919892632d5baaec58)

#### What to build

Create a project using Codex with GPT 5.6. Whether it's an app, agent, website, game, workflow, or something entirely new, your project should fit into one of the following tracks:

*   ##### **Apps for your life**

    *   Consumer apps for everyday life — from productivity and creativity to home, family, travel, health, and personal finance.

*   ##### **Work and productivity**

    *   Tools that make teams faster or more effective, from workflow automation and customer support to analytics, sales, and back-office operations.

*   ##### **Developer tools**

    *   Tools for developers, including testing, DevOps, agentic workflows, and security.

*   ##### Education

    *   Projects that specifically push forward AI for education - either helping students, teachers, or educational organizations.

#### What to submit

*   **A working project.** Build something with Codex using GPT-5.6 that meets the challenge requirements.

*   **A category.**Pick the category that fits best.

*   **A project description.** Tell us what you created and how it works.

*   **A demo video.**Upload a <3-minute public YouTube video showing your project working, audio covering how you used Codex AND GPT-5.6

*   **Provide a URL to your code repository**for judging and testing. The repository must be either public (with relevant licensing) or private and shared with [testing@devpost.com](mailto:testing@devpost.com) and [build-week-event@openai.com](mailto:build-week-event@openai.com).

    *   Include a **README** with setup instructions, sample data (if needed), and clear guidance for running your project

    *   Make sure to **highlight where Codex accelerated your workflow**, where key decisions were made and **how GPT-5.6 and Codex were used**. This is an important part of how judges evaluate technical implementation and quality of the idea.

*   **/feedback Codex Session ID** where the majority of the core functionality where you built your Project, get the /feedback session ID and input it into your submission form

*   **If you're submitting a plugin or developer tool**, also include installation instructions, supported platforms, and a way for judges to test your project without rebuilding it from scratch (such as a demo instance, sandbox, or test account)
